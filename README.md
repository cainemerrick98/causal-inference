### Quasi Experimental Methods
Then I move onto Quasi Experimental methods. Utilising Synthetic Control, Staggered DID...

- Tobacco Tax Policy Evaluation

### Causal ML

- Who Benefits Most? HTE on the IHDP Dataset

- Uplift Modelling for Targeted Marketing Interventions
